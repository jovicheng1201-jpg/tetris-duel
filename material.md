# Milo and the Little Cloud｜繪本素材 Prompt

本文件整理故事中所有主要人物、場景、道具與視覺風格提示詞。建議每次生成圖片時，都固定加入「全書統一風格」與角色的固定特徵，以維持整本繪本的一致性。

## 一、全書統一風格 Prompt

```text
Children’s picture book illustration, warm and gentle storybook atmosphere, soft watercolor and colored pencil texture, hand-painted feeling, rounded shapes, expressive but adorable animal characters, soft natural lighting, cozy woodland palette, muted green, warm golden yellow, sky blue, lavender, and cream, simple clear composition, whimsical details, emotional storytelling, suitable for children ages 4–7, generous empty space for text, high-quality picture book art, consistent character design, no text, no letters, no watermark
```

## 二、負面 Prompt

```text
photorealistic, realistic animal anatomy, scary, frightening, dark horror mood, sharp teeth, aggressive expression, angry face, overly complicated background, messy composition, crowded scene, harsh neon colors, strong black outlines, 3D render, plastic texture, anime style, adult character, extra limbs, duplicate characters, distorted face, deformed body, unreadable text, letters, logo, watermark, speech bubbles
```

## 三、角色設定

### 1. Milo｜小狐狸主角

固定特徵：橘紅色毛皮、奶油色口鼻與胸口、白色尾巴尖端、大而溫柔的棕色眼睛、三角形耳朵、短小的綠色圍巾、棕色小背包。

```text
Milo, a small adorable young fox with warm orange-red fur, cream-colored muzzle and chest, a fluffy tail with a white tip, large kind brown eyes, triangular ears, a small green scarf, and a tiny brown canvas backpack. Curious, gentle, helpful, and cheerful expression. Child-friendly proportions, soft rounded body, charming children’s picture book character design, full body, clean silhouette, soft watercolor and colored pencil texture, warm woodland colors, no text
```

### 2. Puff｜小雲朵主角

固定特徵：小巧、圓滾滾的白色雲朵，邊緣柔軟蓬鬆，帶有淡藍色陰影；表情透過兩個圓眼睛與小嘴巴呈現，不要畫成人形或長手腳。

```text
Puff, a tiny round fluffy white cloud with soft puffy edges, subtle pale blue shadows, two large expressive dark eyes and a small sweet mouth, floating gently in the air. Shy, sensitive, hopeful, and kind expression. No arms and no legs, cloud-shaped body only, adorable children’s picture book character design, soft watercolor and colored pencil texture, warm light, no text
```

### 3. Three Little Birds｜三隻小鳥

固定特徵：三隻體型小巧、顏色不同的森林鳥；一隻黃色、一隻藍色、一隻紅棕色，圓眼睛與短喙，活潑友善。

```text
Three tiny friendly woodland birds helping Milo and Puff: one sunny yellow bird, one sky-blue bird, and one warm reddish-brown bird. Round bodies, small wings, bright curious eyes, tiny beaks, playful and encouraging expressions. Children’s picture book watercolor and colored pencil illustration, soft rounded shapes, no text
```

### 4. Little Deer｜小鹿

固定特徵：淺棕色毛皮、奶油色斑點、大耳朵、細小鹿角或無鹿角皆可，神情溫柔。

```text
A gentle young deer with light caramel-brown fur, soft cream spots, large ears, kind dark eyes, and a delicate friendly face. The deer looks thirsty but hopeful, standing in a woodland clearing. Adorable children’s picture book illustration, watercolor and colored pencil texture, soft natural lighting, no text
```

### 5. Woodland Friends｜森林動物群

```text
A joyful group of woodland animals gathered together: two small rabbits, a gentle young deer, several colorful birds, tiny bees, cheerful frogs, a ladybug, and an old friendly tortoise. All animals have rounded child-friendly shapes and happy expressive faces. Cozy enchanted forest, soft watercolor and colored pencil children’s picture book style, no text
```

### 6. Big Clouds｜大型雲朵

```text
Several large friendly clouds high in a bright blue sky: one cloud shaped vaguely like a gentle dragon, one shaped like a sailing ship, and one small moon-shadow cloud. Soft white and silver edges, peaceful expressions suggested by their shapes, never scary or threatening, whimsical children’s picture book watercolor illustration, no text
```

## 六、角色一致性附加 Prompt

在生成每一張跨頁插圖時，建議將以下文字加在 Prompt 最後：

```text
Keep Milo’s appearance identical in every image: orange-red fur, cream muzzle and chest, white tail tip, green scarf, brown backpack. Keep Puff identical in every image: tiny round fluffy white cloud, pale blue shadows, large dark eyes, small mouth, no arms and no legs. Maintain the same watercolor and colored pencil picture book style, consistent proportions, consistent facial design, and consistent color palette.
```

## 七、繪圖構圖建議

- 封面：Milo 站在森林裡抬頭看 Puff，Puff 位於上方中央，遠處有柔和的彩虹與大雲朵。
- 故事跨頁：角色通常放在畫面中央或下方，保留上方天空或左右兩側的留白給文字。
- 情緒轉折：前半段使用較多藍灰與金黃色；森林缺水時降低色彩飽和度；下雨後恢復明亮的綠色、藍色與彩虹色。
- Puff 的表情：透過眼睛大小、眉毛形狀與嘴巴弧度表現害羞、難過、緊張、驚訝與自信，不要增加人類四肢。
- 所有動物都應保持友善、圓潤與安全感，適合幼兒閱讀。
